## 1. Definitie

**Bron:** Britannica (https://www.britannica.com/topic/epistemology)

> "Epistemology is the branch of philosophy that examines the nature, origin, and limits of knowledge. Also called the theory of knowledge, it explores different types of knowledge, such as propositional knowledge about facts, practical knowledge in the form of skills, and knowledge by acquaintance as a familiarity through experience."

## 2. Klassieke Definitie: Justified True Belief (JTB)

**Bron:** Wikipedia Epistemology + Britannica

> "Since the time of Plato, knowledge has been defined as justified true belief, though disagreements persist about the nature of justification."

> "Knowledge, traditionally defined, is a justified, true belief. However, knowledge requires truth, meaning one cannot 'know' something false."

### Drie voorwaarden voor kennis (JTB-analyse):
1. **Belief** — de persoon gelooft de propositie P
2. **Truth** — propositie P is waar
3. **Justification** — de persoon heeft voldoende rechtvaardiging voor P

**Probleem:** Gettier (1963) toonde aan dat JTB niet voldoende is — Gettier-gevallen zijn situaties waarin iemand iets gerechtvaardigds en waars gelooft maar het niet 'weet'.

## 4. Hoofdstromingen

**Bron:** WebSearch → Britannica + Wikipedia

> "Empiricists hold that all knowledge comes from sense experience, whereas rationalists believe that some knowledge does not depend on it."

> "The school of skepticism questions the human ability to attain knowledge, while fallibilism says that knowledge is never certain."

> "Coherentists argue that a belief is justified if it is consistent with other beliefs. Internalism and externalism debate whether justification is determined solely by mental states or also by external circumstances."

### Stromingen:
| Stroming | Kernstelling |
|---|---|
| **Empirisme** | Alle kennis komt uit zintuiglijke ervaring (Hume, Locke) |
| **Rationalisme** | Sommige kennis is a priori, onafhankelijk van ervaring (Descartes, Leibniz) |
| **Scepticisme** | We kunnen niets met zekerheid weten |
| **Fallibilisme** | Kennis is nooit zeker, altijd reviseerbaar (Peirce, Popper) |
| **Coherentisme** | Rechtvaardiging = coherentie met andere overtuigingen |
| **Internalisme** | Rechtvaardiging bepaald door mentale toestanden |
| **Externalisme** | Rechtvaardiging mede bepaald door externe omstandigheden (Goldman's reliabilisme) |
