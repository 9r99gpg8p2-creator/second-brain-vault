## Definitie (directe quotes/parafrasen)

- "Alexithymia is defined as the inability of persons to describe their emotional states, to identify the feelings of others, and a utilitarian type of thinking." (PMC6124373)
- "A multifaceted construct comprising: (1) difficulty identifying feelings, (2) difficulty describing feelings, (3) externally oriented thinking." (TAS-20 construct)
- Term bedacht door Peter Sifneos (1973): Grieks — a (geen) + lexis (woord) + thymos (gevoel/gemoed)

## Prevalentie

- Algemene bevolking: ~10%
- Autisme spectrum: verhoogd (>40% comorbiditeit)
- PTSS/oorlogsveteranen: ~41%
- Depressieve stoornissen: ~50%
- Eetstoornissen: significant verhoogd
- Verslaving: prevalent, bijdraagt aan risico

## Neurobiologische basis

- **Insulaire cortex**: kerngebied voor interoceptieve verwerking; bij alexithymie gedysreguleerd
- "Insula is linked to functions of interoceptive awareness, self-awareness and social emotions" (PMC3621096)
- "High degree of alexithymia was associated with a dysfunction in the insula"
- **Anterieure cingulate cortex (ACC)**: betrokken bij affectieve verwerking en zelfregulatie
- "Enhanced neural activity in somatosensory and sensorimotor regions, including the insula"
- "Neural activity in medial, prefrontal, and insula cortex was lowered when people with alexithymia were involved in social tasks"
- EEG-onderzoek bevestigt altered neural correlates (PMC11943194)

## Meetinstrument: TAS-20

- Toronto Alexithymia Scale (TAS-20) — meest gebruikte zelfrapportage
- 3 subschalen: (1) DIF: Difficulty Identifying Feelings, (2) DDF: Difficulty Describing Feelings, (3) EOT: Externally Oriented Thinking
- Meta-analyse CFA bevestigt driefactorstructuur (PMC9597132)

## Transdiagnostisch karakter

- "Increased levels of alexithymia represent a transdiagnostic marker across psychiatric and psychosomatic entities" 
- Aanwezig bij: ASS, PTSS, depressie, eetstoornissen, verslaving, persoonlijkheidsstoornissen
- Verbinding met interoceptie: "Recent findings suggest that interoception is a fundamental component of emotional experience and that impaired interoception is associated with alexithymia"
- "Alexithymia, not autism, is associated with atypical interoception" (PMC4962768) — belangrijk onderscheid

## Primaire vs. Secundaire Alexithymia

- **Primaire**: constitutioneel, neurobiologisch van oorsprong
- **Secundaire**: als gevolg van trauma, PTSS, of andere psychopathologie
